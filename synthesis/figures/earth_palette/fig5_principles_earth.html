3Right capital at the right stage

4Repayment follows the season
Seasonal schedules, low-season grace and order-linked repayment are standard features, not exceptions

5Minimum concessionality, tapering
First loss, fee and price support only where it unlocks lending that would not otherwise happen, falling on a pre-agreed schedule

6Inclusion by design
Women-, youth- and community-led first, without rigid thresholds; one youth band (18–35); collateral they hold; local languages, paper forms, cash and mobile money

7Coaching before and after finance
Records, costing and compliance before disbursement; support for the life of the loan, with buyers or mentors following up

8Formalization as a milestone
Registration and tax clearance supported when a buyer or lender requires them, not demanded at entry

9One measurement system
Baseline, grant milestones, RTFF step-downs and results framework use the same disaggregated GM1-D5 indicators
