1
Supply MOU
Written orders, payment within 30 days (15 for micro suppliers), notice before termination, optional buyer-deducted repayment.
2
RTFF referral and co-financing protocol
Eligibility screen, one-page profile, decision deadline, coded rejection reasons, grant as borrower contribution, seasonal schedule, in-country recovery.
3
Grant manual
Lots, eligibility, scoring (market linkage 25%, readiness 20%, R-NBT 20%, inclusion 20%, financing plan 15%), tranches, conversion clause, safeguards.
4
Common readiness diagnostic
Intake fields from F6-D9 Annex C, returning a tier (0–3) and a grant-ready, guarantee-ready or loan-ready tag.
